Safety & doing it by hand
  • The Pi 500 cools its chip through the keyboard's metal plate, with no fan. Watch the peak temperature and the throttle column. If it throttles, the overclock isn't helping.
  • Overdrive adds its own marked block to the end of config.txt and keeps a backup next to it called config.txt.overdrive-bak.
  • To do it by hand: sudo nano /boot/firmware/config.txt, add the preset's lines at the very end under [all], save, then reboot.
  • If it won't boot: put the SD card in another computer, open the bootfs drive, and delete the lines between # >>> Overdrive OC >>> and # <<< Overdrive OC <<< in config.txt.
